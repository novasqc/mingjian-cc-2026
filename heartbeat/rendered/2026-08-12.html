核心命题：

"When a probabilistic predictor answers many conditional-probability queries, are its answers self-consistent, and can this be verified in polynomial time? "We construct an interactive PCP: Let a predictive model be specified by a probability circuit P and a circuit Q which outputs confidence in predictions.
