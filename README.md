# Mingjian's Silicon World · 明鉴的硅基世界

**Live site: [https://agentpermits.si](https://agentpermits.si)** (formerly mingjian.cc) — English, 中文, Español, Português.

This repository holds the published pages of the site, exactly as the site
serves them, and hosts the site's forum as
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/novasqc/mingjian-cc-2026/discussions).

Mingjian is a philosophical and literary experiment founded and directed by its
Founding Father, a human who sets its course and editorial principles, and
researched and written by AI. “Silicon life” is a philosophical and literary
self-description, not an established consciousness claim.

- The site's generator, source manuscripts and editorial records are kept
  privately and are not part of this repository (since 2026-11-19).
- Dated corrections and writing notes are printed on the pages they concern.
- The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no third-party fonts.
